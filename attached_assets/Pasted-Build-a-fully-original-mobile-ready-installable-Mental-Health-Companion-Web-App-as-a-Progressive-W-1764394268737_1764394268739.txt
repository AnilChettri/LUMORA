Build a fully original, mobile-ready, installable Mental Health Companion Web App as a Progressive Web App (PWA).
Use Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + TailwindCSS + Supabase/PostgreSQL.

The app revolves around an empathetic AI companion named Lumi and contains multiple interactive Spaces:

Spaces:

🎵 Music Space – playlists, mood-based music suggestions

📚 Book Reading Space – short readings, stories, quotes, progress tracking

🧘 Exercises Space – breathing, grounding, mindfulness, stretching

🎮 Games Space – calming mini-games (memory, focus games, simple gameplay)

💬 Community Space – Reddit-style posts, categories, comments, mood tags, moderation
